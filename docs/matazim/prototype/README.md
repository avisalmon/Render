## The design system, as drawn

- **Teal** is identity: wordmark, hero heading, completed states, the left half
  of the gradient. **Purple** is action: primary buttons, the current-stage
  accents, the right half of the gradient. **Dark blue** is the secondary
  action. Dark navy for text. This resolves the old contrast problem cleanly:
  teal never has to carry small text or button labels.
- White cards on a very light grey page, generous radius, soft shadows, wide
  spacing.
- A teal-to-purple gradient band used once, for the stats.
- Illustrated 3D objects (cube, robot, laptop, trophy, flag) as section marks.
- Decorative purple blobs and dotted arcs behind the hero.
- Progress is shown three ways and all three appear: rings on cards, a bar for
  the overall figure, a donut in the sidebar.
- Status is always a word plus a colour, never a colour alone.
- Photos of real teenagers, and a real photo as the member avatar.

## What these screens decide that the written brief did not

1. **Two front doors**, כניסת תלמידים and כניסת מובילים, chosen before login.
2. **The entrance test is public**, a hero CTA and a nav item, reachable before
   any account exists.
3. **Nav differs logged out and logged in**, and המסלול השנתי becomes המסלול שלי.
4. **Courses are stage-grouped and locked**, with an explicit advance rule shown
   as השלימו עוד 2 קורסים כדי לעבור לשלב הבא.
5. **פרקטיקום is a course category**, not only something tracked.
6. **The track is a sequence of typed milestones**, each with its own status and
   date. This is much more than a five-value status field.
7. **Notifications exist**: a bell, a mail icon, and a משוב חדש card.
8. **Student work is published publicly**, attributed to a school.
9. **The public funnel is four stages**, not five.
10. **Intel is named** as a partner in the hero, and the audience is stated as
    כיתות ט'.

## Inconsistencies in the mockups themselves

- The submissions section is called **התוצרים שלי** in the nav on screen 2 and
  **הגשות ותוצרים** on screen 3. Same section, two names. Pick one.
- Screen 2 gives the member an initials avatar, screen 3 a photograph. Also two
  answers to one question, and the safer default is initials (REQ-M.30a).
- The milestone legend on screen 3 is drawn at a size that does not survive the
  screenshot. Five states are clearly there and four are legible; the fifth is a
  guess. Confirm the wording with Litala before building the status set.

## What worries me in them

- **The numbers.** 1,250 students, 120 leaders, 28 schools. Our scoping said 20
  to 40 teenagers per cohort, and the whole design rests on certification being
  scarce and granted by hand by two people. If these figures are real rather
  than filler, hand-granting does not scale and school leaders must carry it.
- **The public gallery.** Minors' project work, published with school
  attribution. No student is named on the cards, which is the right instinct,
  but publishing needs an explicit opt-in and a staff review step.
- **The photo avatar.** A real face for a ninth-grader. Initials, as on screen
  2, are the safer default.
