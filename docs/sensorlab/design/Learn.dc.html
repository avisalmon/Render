What actually falls?

Gravity pulls harder on a heavier object — but it also has more mass to move. The two cancel, so everything accelerates at the same rate: g.

The relationship
h = ½ g t² g = 2h / t²
Measure the drop height and the time to land, and g falls out. Your phone times the fall far more precisely than you can.
Watch for this. The accelerometer does not measure how fast the phone moves. It measures the forces pushing on it.
