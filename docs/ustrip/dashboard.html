Sprint 11 — The fix sprint
Proposed

13 findings from the 2026-09-14 review. P1: no offline/PWA, photos upload at full size onto a 1GB disk shared with the database, 20-26px tap targets in a phone-first app, no double-submit guard. P2: API N+1 (425 queries on one endpoint) and no phone guard test.

Open items
Family access: Avi still needs to add Nirit and the kids to the family group in /admin/ — the app is live but locked for everyone except Avi (superuser) until then.
Offline: no manifest, no service worker. Lose signal in the Finger Lakes and the family loses the itinerary — the one thing this app exists to hold. Sprint 11 F1.
Photos vs. the disk: full-size phone photos (3-8MB) upload untouched onto a 1GB Render disk that also holds the SQLite database. Sprint 11 F2.
JournalPost.author cascade: deleting a family member's account deletes their journal posts with it (CASCADE), unlike packing's SET_NULL. Flagged for a yes/no, still not decided.
Backup window: photos are in the weekly GCS backup, so up to 7 days of the trip's photos sit unbacked at any moment. Sprint 11 F12 — a decision, not obviously a build.
