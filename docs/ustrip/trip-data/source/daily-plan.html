Day 6 — Wed Sep 23 · Niagara FallsSleeping: Canadian side▸
  • —Plan Drive ~2.5 hrs to Niagara Falls.
  • —Plan Niagara Falls State Park (US side) — bring a change of socks/shoes, you will get soaked.
  • —Plan Cave of the Winds — $23/person, includes a poncho; buy at the park, timed elevator entry.
  • 9:00–17:00Plan Maid of the Mist boat to the base of the falls — $30.25/person, no fixed time slot (boats every 15 min); buy a few days ahead to skip the line.
  • —Plan Cross Rainbow Bridge to the Canadian side, check in.
  • EveningPlan Niagara Parkway — panoramic view of all three falls; Clifton Hill lights and attractions; falls illuminated at night + fireworks. Bring Canadian dollars — USD is accepted but at a poor rate, no change given in USD.
