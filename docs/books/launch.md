# Books: the first launch checklist

The pilot is one real circle in Haifa or Tel Aviv with enough books between
its members that asking is worth it (spec 12.1, BK6). Everything the code
needs is live; what is left is people. Tick in order.

## Before inviting anyone

- [ ] ACT-BK.4: choose the circle (a book club, school, workplace or
      neighbourhood) and its first 10 to 20 people. One cluster, not two.
- [ ] ACT-BK.2: a lawyer's read on the privacy page, the terms, the
      reading-log privacy (Amendment 13) and the no-money-flow rule.
- [ ] ACT-BK.1: AnyBook's founders, why the consumer app gave way to kiosks.
- [ ] ACT-BK.7: the National Library key, so Hebrew spines match records
      instead of staying "as read".
- [ ] Avi runs one real shelf scan on production (checks the OpenAI key on
      Render and starts the in-app accuracy count).
- [ ] Avi opens the circle at https://babook.co.il/books/circles/ (private),
      sets its area, adds a partner meeting place if one agreed.

## The scanning evening

- [ ] Everyone signs up and onboards (name, area, 18+, terms) before the
      evening, on their own phones, from the invite link.
- [ ] One wide photo per shelf, straight on, books standing, whole shelf in
      frame; the review screen is where the misreads get fixed.
- [ ] Each person marks at least five books as offered (lend is the easy
      one). The circle page shows the count climbing.
- [ ] Target before the hub "opens" for the circle: a few hundred books.

## The first week

- [ ] Avi reads https://babook.co.il/books/pilot/ daily: the funnel says
      where people stop; feedback says why.
- [ ] Every open report and dispute is answered by a person within a day.
- [ ] The "done when" of BK6: one loan and its return, and one exchange,
      completed through the app between two people who did not know each
      other.

## What the numbers must show before a second circle

- Scanned to offering: more than half of the people who photographed a
  shelf offer at least one book.
- Asked to completed: more than half of approved requests reach a handover.
- Model cost per person under a shekel a month.
