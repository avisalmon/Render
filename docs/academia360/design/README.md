# Academia360 design

## Round 1 (2026-11-26): rejected

Three directions (A institutional, B editorial, C civic) on the same three
screens. Avi: boring, standard, "AI made this": stat tiles, same-looking
buttons, the same template in three colours. Kept here as the record of
what not to do. Canvas (private): https://claude.ai/artifact/UjvVvwea7ZTnh1nKvMeLv9

## What exists on the web under "אקדמיה 360" (checked 2026-11-26)

No official national site. מל"ג has only the decision page. Institutions
have news pages. Two things worth taking:

- **Campus IL** (campus.gov.il/municipality/academy-360): Avi's pick as the
  lead. Israel's national digital campus. Dark charcoal top bar, organic
  colour shapes (teal, yellow, coral, green) as background texture, pill
  buttons, white cards with a 10px radius and a soft shadow, a page title
  in a white rounded box over the hero, Assistant as the typeface, a lot of
  white.
- **Haifa's "אקדמיה 360"** site: the only thing that looks like program
  branding, a 360 inside a circular arrow.
- **The teaching-centres forum** page: one deep colour, real black-and-white
  photographs, quiet type.

### Campus IL tokens (from its public stylesheets, for reference only)

Palette: black `#323333`, white `#FCFCFC`, green `#91C653`, blue (teal)
`#70C6D1`, yellow `#FDCC09`, red (coral) `#F15859`; greys in steps from
`#E7EBEF` to `#191A1A`; tints of each colour (`green-light-1` `#F7FBF2` ...).
Type: Assistant (Regular, Medium, Bold). Radii: 10px on cards and inputs,
25 to 30px on pill buttons, 50% on avatars.

We borrow the language, not the files: our own palette in our own tokens,
no copying of their CSS, illustrations or logo.
