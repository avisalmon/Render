    pytest -q --tb=no -p no:randomly > /tmp/run.txt
    grep -a '^FAILED' /tmp/run.txt | sed 's/ - .*//' | sort > /tmp/now.txt
    comm -13 docs/regression_baseline.txt /tmp/now.txt   # anything here is yours

growing it silently is not. Regenerate the list when you do fix some, and
