Say it in words

Write what you need, with the sizes you know. The chat fills in the rest and tells you what it assumed.

A desk organizer with three round slots for pens and one slot for my phone. About 120 mm wide. Rounded edges.

Or draw it

Draw it on paper and write the sizes on it, in millimetres. Take a photo with the camera button next to the text box.

A box like this.

Ten seconds later: it read every size on the drawing, and asked where exactly the holes should go.

{% elif step == 4 %}

Or photograph the real thing

Need a holder, a stand or a cover for something? Photograph it. Put a bank card or a coin next to it, so the chat can tell its size.

Build a cup holder

Here's a freestanding holder with a wide base for stability. Could you measure the cup's height, bottom diameter and widest diameter? Version 1 · ready to print

It built a first holder at once from its best estimate, then asked for the real sizes.

{% elif step == 5 %}

Change it by talking. Point at it.

Ask for changes in plain words. To say where, tap Point and tap the part: a blue dot marks the spot.

Make the wall thicker herepoint marked on the part

Tap Measure and tap two points to see the distance.

{% elif step == 6 %}

It checks the print. You download.

Every version is checked for a 3D printer: it lies flat, nothing floats, no wall is too thin, it fits the bed. If something is wrong, Fix it asks the chat to correct it.

When you like it, tap Done under the chat. The STL opens in any slicer, and you can keep changing it after you download.

design3d is for simple functional parts. A full case for electronics with several boards is better finished in Fusion 360 or SolidWorks, from the files you download here.
