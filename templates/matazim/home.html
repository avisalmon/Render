{% comment %} SPR-M.42, replacing REQ-M.5c's two doors. This page used to open by asking the reader which kind of person they are: a disabled כניסת תלמידים behind a padlock, a solid כניסת מובילים beside it, and the entrance test third, as an outline. Avi, on reading the SPR-M.41 measurement of that: the site should not put anybody on the spot like that, it is not pleasant, and the weight was pointing at adults on a page written to recruit fourteen-year-olds. So there is one path here now, and it belongs to the teenager the product is for. Whoever lands here is assumed to be one. A leader is never asked either. They arrive through their own invite link, which already carries the fact that they are a leader, and the door for a teacher who has no link yet stays in the menu: מבחן הכניסה is not in their way, and כניסת מובילים has been a nav item since REQ-M.103. Seen, but not in front of your eyes. {% endcomment %}
