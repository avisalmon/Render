improv

Practise piano improvisation over a backing band, and hear how you did.

What you get

  • A backing band in any key, over chord charts from blues to bossa.
  • Lessons and challenges that listen to your piano and tell you what to fix.
  • Scale and chord trainers with the fingering shown.
  • Your progress, your streak and your best takes.

It is free, for everyone. It works best in Chrome or Edge with a digital piano or MIDI keyboard on USB. Without one you can still look around.

This is an early version. After you try it, please tell us what you think: there is a Feedback link on every screen.
