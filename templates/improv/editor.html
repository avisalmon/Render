      How to write a chart
      
    
        
  • Bars sit between bar lines: | Dm7 | G7 | Cmaj7 | % |. A % repeats the bar before it.
    • 
        
  • Two or more chords in a bar share it evenly: | Dm7 G7 |.
    • 
        
  • Slash chords keep their bass note: C/E. Dm7, Dmin7 and D-7 are the same chord.
    • 
        
  • Repeats: |: A | B :|. Endings: |: A | [1 B :| [2 C |.
    • 
        
  • A key change sits between bars: {key: Eb}. A line starting with // is a comment.
    •
