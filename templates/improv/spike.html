Timing spike

Proves three things on your own piano and laptop before anything else is built on them: your piano's notes arrive, a click plays out, and the page can say how far each note landed from the click. Use Chrome or Edge, and keep this tab in front while you play.

1. Start

Browsers only start sound after a click. Press the button, then turn the piano on and plug it in by USB. If the browser asks to use your MIDI devices, say yes. Without MIDI the on-screen keyboard below still works, but its timing is not trustworthy.

Not started.

2. Click

Play one note on each click, as steadily as you can, for a minute. Use the lowest volume setting on the piano's own speakers, or the headphone socket, so you hear the click and not the piano's sound on top of it.

3. Keyboard

Lights from the piano and from the mouse or touch. The two are told apart in the table.

Sustain pedal: up

4. Result

Play some notes with the click running.

Positive means after the click, negative means before it.
