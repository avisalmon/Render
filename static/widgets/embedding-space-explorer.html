Words as points in space

Similar words sit close together. And the arrow between two words is a direction of meaning you can reuse: A minus B plus C lands near a new word.

− +

Left to right there is a gender direction; the same arrow moves man to woman and king to queen. That is why king − man + woman lands on queen. The countries have their own "capital of" direction. Real embeddings do this in hundreds of dimensions and across millions of words; this is a hand-placed 2D sketch so the geometry is easy to see.
