Softmax explorer

Drag the three scores and the temperature. Softmax turns any scores into probabilities that add up to 1.

temperature: below 1 sharpens, above 1 flattens

Softmax exponentiates each score and divides by the total, so a slightly higher score becomes a lot more probable. Raising one score steals probability from the others; temperature sharpens or flattens the whole distribution. The same softmax powers the training loss, attention, and word sampling.
